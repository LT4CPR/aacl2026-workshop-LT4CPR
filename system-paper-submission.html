Paper Format

  • Length: up to eight (8) pages of main content, plus references and appendices.
  • Style: use the official ACL style files and do not modify the required formatting.
  • File format: submit the paper as a PDF through OpenReview.
  • Anonymity: the review version should be anonymized for double-blind review. Do not include author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or other identifying information in the review PDF.
  • Appendices: appendices should appear after the references. The main paper should remain self-contained.

What the System Paper Should Describe

The paper should provide enough detail for readers to understand how your submitted system was built and how it produced the official shared-task outputs. At minimum, please describe the following where applicable:

  • the overall approach and system architecture;
  • models, training procedures, prompts, retrieval components, and other major system components;
  • task data and any external data or resources used;
  • external APIs, pretrained models, retrieval sources, and manual interventions;
  • experimental setup and results;
  • error analysis or qualitative analysis of system behavior;
  • limitations and known failure modes; and
  • ethical considerations and responsible-use considerations relevant to crisis-related data and generated situation reports.
Transparency requirement. Please clearly disclose external data, pretrained models, APIs, prompts, retrieval sources, and any human or manual intervention used in producing the submitted system outputs.

Submission Checklist

  • Your team participated in the LT4CPR Shared Task.
  • The paper describes the system associated with your shared-task participation.
  • The review PDF is anonymized.
  • The paper uses the official ACL style files.
  • The main content does not exceed 8 pages.
  • References and any appendices follow the main paper.
  • The paper documents external resources and manual interventions used by the system.
  • The PDF is submitted to the dedicated LT4CPR Shared Task OpenReview venue before the deadline.
