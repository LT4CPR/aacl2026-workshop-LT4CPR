Why the remote talks sit where they do

The venue is eight hours ahead of London and thirteen ahead of the US east coast, and none of the zones involved are on summer time by 9 November, so every offset below is fixed. Opening the day with the remote session puts the Americas-based presenters in their own evening rather than the middle of the night. Late afternoon at the venue is morning in Europe and Africa, which is why the Italian and Nigerian talks sit at the end of the day instead. The two remote shared-task talks run at 10 minutes and sit back to back, so the remote setup is only needed once.
