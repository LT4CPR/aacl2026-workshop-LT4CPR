# LinkedIn post draft — LT4CPR 2026

 How can the *CL community support crisis and humanitarian technology? Join us on **9 November** for **LT4CPR 2026 — Language Technologies for Crisis Preparedness and Response**, co-located with AACL-IJCNLP 2026!

🎤 **Keynote: Professor Federico M. Federici, University College London**

Building human-centred approaches to multilingual crisis communication, connecting communities, language technologies, and sustainable workflows across preparedness, response, and recovery.

🌍 **A perspective from humanitarian practice: Milena Haykowska, CLEAR Global**

Drawing on work with community linguists and the example of Rohingya, Milena will present how human input can help voice technologies meet real needs in emergencies—and why communities must help shape their development and evaluation.

The programme also includes research presentations, a shared task on crisis situation reports, and a discussion connecting NLP research with humanitarian practice.

📍 **Room MR 4, Hengqin, Zhuhai, China + online**

📅 **Monday, 9 November 2026**

We look forward to bringing researchers and practitioners together!
