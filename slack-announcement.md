🌍 We are excited to announce the **First Workshop on Language Technologies for Crisis Preparedness and Response (LT4CPR)**, co-located with AACL-IJCNLP 2026, on November 9, 2026 in Hengqin, Zhuhai, China. The workshop will be held in **hybrid format**, so you can join in person or online 🌐

When language AI meets crisis operations, lives can be saved.

🚨 The language technologies applied in response to the 2010 Haitian earthquake saved thousands of lives. This year alone, earthquakes in Venezuela and Colombia (along with several minor aftershocks) have been a reminder of how often these events strike regions where language and information barriers can slow response. In a world hit by more than 350 disasters per year, most of the severest crises occur where English is not spoken. With the rapid growth of AI, the potential of language technologies in disaster preparedness and response has never been greater.

LT4CPR aims to bring together NLP researchers and crisis management stakeholders (practitioners, NGOs, and industry) to develop interdisciplinary 🤝 solutions with real-world impact.

We welcome submissions on:

- 🔍 AI for crisis response (information extraction, situational awareness, automated triage)
- 🗣️ Multilingual and multimodal research for humanitarian initiatives
- 🛠️ Translating LT research into operational tools
- 👥 Qualitative studies to understand community needs
- ⚖️ Ethics and societal implications of AI in crisis communication
- 📋 Field reports from practitioners and humanitarian organizations
